Getting around · JR Pass & transit ✓ Verified Jun 24, 2026

🧮
A nationwide JR Pass probably isn't worth it
14-day Ordinary pass = ¥80,000 (valid thru Sep 30 '26). Point-to-point fares for this one-way routing total only ~¥57–58k — so a nationwide pass loses you ~¥22k. Buy tickets per leg, or grab the regional pass below for the back half.
🎟️
Better buy: JR-West Kansai–Hiroshima Area Pass
¥17,000 adult / ¥8,500 child · 5 consecutive days. Covers the Sanyo Shinkansen Shin-Osaka↔Hiroshima (incl. Nozomi/Mizuho), the JR Miyajima ferry, all Kansai-area JR (Osaka · Kyoto · Kobe · Nara · Himeji) and the Amanohashidate line. Hiroshima→Osaka alone is ~¥10,500 one-way, plus every Kyoto/Osaka JR hop and the ferry — so it’s break-even to a clear win for the back half, and far cheaper than the ¥80k nationwide pass. (Doesn’t cover Tokyo, Fuji or Narita — buy those separately.)
✅
On a JR Pass (if you hold one)
All Hikari / Sakura Shinkansen · the N'EX to/from Narita · JR Sagano line → Arashiyama · JR Nara line → Fushimi Inari (local trains only) · JR Sanyo → Miyajimaguchi + the JR Miyajima ferry.
⛔
Not covered — budget cash / IC
Tokyo Metro (Tozai → Nishikasai & TeamLab) · Fujikyu Otsuki→Kawaguchiko ¥1,770/pax each way · Kyoto city buses (Kinkaku-ji) · Kintetsu/Hanshin. Nozomi/Mizuho need a ¥4,170–¥6,500 supplement — but free Sakura/Hikari are only 5–20 min slower, so skip it.
⛩️
Miyajima island tax
¥100 per person visitor tax (since Oct 2024), collected with the ferry fare. Not on the pass.
✈️
Flight day Jul 21 — the tight one
ZIPAIR check-in closes 6:10 PM — target NRT by ~16:30. Per JR Central's 2026-03-14 timetable, Hikari run ~2/hr (~2h40 Kyoto→Tokyo). From Kyoto: take the ~12:30 Hikari → Tokyo ~15:10 → N'EX 15:33 → NRT 16:31. Last-safe is the N'EX 16:33 → NRT 17:29, so don't leave Kyoto past ~13:30. Good to know

🏨
Hotel front desks hold bags free
Before check-in and after checkout at Lumiere (Tokyo), Loisir (Kyoto), and via the Osaka host — always ask, it's standard and free.
🧳
Crosta is Kyoto-specific
Kyoto Station's staffed hands-free counter (B1). Major stations have equivalents (ecbo cloak, station lockers); Kawaguchiko has nothing — plan the Fuji bag storage around that.
🚄
Shinkansen oversized-baggage seat — Jul 15 & Jul 21
Free reservation required for cases over 160 cm total (a big checked suitcase sits right on the line). Grab the back-row "oversized" seats when buying tickets — ¥1,000 onboard fee if you skip it. (The JR Special Rapid on Jul 18 has no such rule.)
🔐
Coin lockers
S ¥300–400 · M ¥500–600 · L ¥600–800 · Suica/IC tap-to-lock at most stations. Big-station lockers fill by mid-morning — Crosta is the reliable fallback.
🚕
Download the Go app before you fly
Japan's Uber-equivalent for taxis — add a card at home. It's the safety net for the two move days where street-hailing fails: the Kawaguchiko villa (Jul 15) and the Nishinari Airbnb (Jul 21, the taxi that protects your NRT flight). On move days a taxi with the bags beats dragging four cases through the metro — keep the metro route as the fallback only.

Data outside WiFi · which eSIM

📱 eSIM — Airalo

Install before you fly at airalo.com. Japan & the US need different coverage — two ways to do it:

Cheapest — two country eSIMs / phone
  • Japan — “Moshi Moshi” 10 GB / 15 days ≈ $17.50 (fits the ~13-day Japan leg).
  • Hawaii/US — “United States” 5 GB ≈ $16 (6 days; switch the eSIM when you land).
  • ≈ $34/phone → ~$170 for all five.
Simplest — one eSIM, no switching
  • “Discover Global” 10 GB ≈ $59 works Japan + US automatically (≈ $295 for five).
  • Save more: put eSIMs on 2 phones (bump to 20 GB) and hotspot the other three.
Light use (maps/chat/rideshare) ~5 GB/region is plenty; heavy → 20 GB. Prices ~mid-2026 — check live rates & validity at airalo.com.

Alternatives & WiFi

📶 Other data options

  • Skip the eSIM in Japan? NRT SIM counters + vending machines, T1 & T2, 24/7 (¥2,000–6,000).
  • Hawaii/US: or add your carrier's US roaming day-pass (Rogers/Bell/Telus ~$12–15/day) — pricey over 6 days.
  • WiFi at all hotels + the villa, plus the Newark & Dulles layovers — fine for messaging.
  • One shared pocket WiFi in Japan is an alternative to five individual eSIMs.
